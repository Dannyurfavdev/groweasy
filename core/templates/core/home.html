Where Construction Communication Actually Breaks Down

Every project starts organized, schedules are clear and scopes are understood.

Everyone knows the plan.

Then the job begins. Photos start coming in through WhatsApp, voice notes get sent from the field, drive folders grow and sheets get updated.

Someone forwards an RFI.

Someone misses the update.

Nothing is technically “lost”.

But the information becomes scattered across conversations, folders, and people.

By the time leadership sees the full picture, the field has already adapted.

Crews move, Schedules tighten and Decisions get made with partial context.

Most construction problems aren’t caused by bad decisions.

They’re caused by decisions made without visibility.

GrowEasy Analytics exists to fix that.
